Privacy policy

App: Personal Shorts Uploader
Operator: mingmoauto
Effective: 2026-11-06

This is a personal tool used only by its operator to upload the operator's own produced videos to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Data and purpose

With the operator's authorization, the tool reads the operator's YouTube channel identity and list of uploaded videos (to confirm the correct channel and avoid duplicate uploads), uploads private videos, and sets their metadata and thumbnails. It does not access any other user's data.

Processing and storage

Processing runs in the operator's private cloud development environment. The OAuth refresh token is stored only in that private environment and is never published. Upload IDs are recorded in the operator's private repository to prevent duplicates. This public page contains no credentials or private video data.

Sharing and use limits

Data is sent only to Google APIs for the authorized YouTube operations. The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

The operator can revoke access at any time in Google Account permissions and delete the stored token. Uploaded videos remain in the operator's YouTube account until the operator deletes them.

Contact the operator through the information repository.
