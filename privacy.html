Privacy policy

App: 밍모 YouTube 자동 업로드
Operator: mingmoauto
Effective: 2026-10-21

This is a personal tool used only by its operator to transfer the operator's authorized edited videos from Google Drive to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Data and purpose

The tool reads authorized Drive folder and file metadata, video/audio files, and companion music credit documents. It reads the operator's YouTube channel and upload status and creates unlisted videos with the original filename title, preserved music credits and links, and relevant tags. Audio recognition runs locally on the workflow runner; no paid or external generative AI service receives the audio or transcript.

Processing and storage

Processing takes place on GitHub-hosted Actions runners. Google OAuth credentials are stored in encrypted GitHub Actions repository secrets. Upload IDs, resumable upload session information and prepared titles/descriptions/tags are stored in the operator's private repository to prevent duplicates and recover interrupted transfers. Temporary video/audio files are removed after successful processing; the hosted runner is discarded after its job. This public information website contains no credentials, private videos or upload ledger.

Sharing and use limits

Data is sent to Google APIs for the authorized Drive and YouTube operations and processed using GitHub infrastructure for this workflow. The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

The operator can stop scheduled processing through the repository automation setting, revoke access in Google Account permissions, and remove the repository secrets and stored upload records. Uploaded videos remain in the operator's YouTube account until the operator deletes them. Original files remain in Google Drive; this tool does not delete them.

Contact

Contact the operator through the information repository. Do not include passwords, tokens or private video data in public issues.
